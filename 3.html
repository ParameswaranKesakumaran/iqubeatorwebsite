HERA Nigeria Nigerian Women Founders Scaling Globally

From survival to strategy. From Lagos to the world

Executive Summary

HERA Nigeria is a 3-week intensive, trauma-informed, cycle-aware accelerator designed specifically for Nigerian women and women-identifying founders aiming to scale their ventures to North America.

The program equips founders with:

  • Practical business and startup skills aligned with global standards (YC, Techstars, DMZ)
  • Cycle-aware and trauma-informed methodology
  • North American market exposure and investor readiness
  • Ongoing mentorship, support and post-program acceleration

Problem Statement

Challenges faced by Nigerian women founders:

  • Only ~12% of Nigerian startups are women-led
  • African women-led startups raised just US$209M (2019–2023)
  • Less than 10% accessed global investors or diaspora funding
  • Limited mentorship, trauma barriers & systemic gender bias

Gap HERA Nigeria Addresses:

  • High-quality global startup training for women founders
  • Cycle-aware & trauma-informed leadership development
  • Access to North American investors and diaspora networks
  • A culturally sensitive, safe learning ecosystem

Program Structure

  • Duration: 3-Week Intensive + 3-Month Post-Program Acceleration
  • Delivery Mode: Hybrid (Virtual + Optional North America Immersion)
  • Daily Commitment: 2–3 hours + weekly 1:1 coaching

Key Principles

  • Cycle-Aware Learning: Align challenging work with follicular/ovulation phases; reflection & integration with menstrual/luteal phases
  • Trauma-Informed: Address cultural silencing, survival-mode patterns, and intergenerational trauma
  • Global Scale Orientation: Prepare founders for North American investor expectations, market entry, and diaspora collaboration


Cycle-Aware Curriculum

Menstrual (Inner Winter)

Days: 1–5

Strengths: Reflection, intuition, emotional clarity

Avoid: High-pressure pitching

Program Alignment: Storytelling, identity circles, gentle workshops

Follicular (Inner Spring)

Days: 6–12

Strengths: Energy, planning, idea generation

Avoid: Overcommitment

Program Alignment: Business model design, strategy, brainstorming

Ovulation (Inner Summer)

Days: 13–18

Strengths: Visibility, collaboration, leadership

Avoid: Over-talking, burnout

Program Alignment: Pitch practice, networking, NA market simulations

Luteal (Inner Autumn)

Days: 19–28

Strengths: Focus, finishing tasks, operations

Avoid: Starting new things

Program Alignment: Branding, GTM execution, financial clarity





Detailed Weekly Curriculum

Week 1 – Identity, Power & Business Foundations (Menstrual → Follicular)

  • Trauma-informed founder psychology
  • Story & identity reclamation
  • Value proposition & business model design
  • Customer discovery (local & diaspora focus)

Deliverables: Founder Identity Statement, Business Model Canvas, Customer Profiles

Week 2 – Go-To-Market & North American Expansion (Follicular → Ovulation)

  • Brand story & positioning for diaspora & North American audiences
  • GTM strategy for local and global markets
  • Sales, partnerships, negotiation & boundary skills
  • Collaboration with diaspora networks and mentors

Deliverables: Brand Messaging Kit, 3-Month GTM Plan, Sales & Partnership Pipeline

Week 3 – Investment Readiness & Leadership (Ovulation → Luteal)

  • Startup finance, revenue modeling, and valuation
  • Investor landscape: North American & diaspora networks
  • Pitch architecture, media, and visibility
  • Demo Day simulation with investor feedback

Deliverables: Investor-ready pitch deck, Funding plan & ask, Demo Day presentation

Post-Program Acceleration

  • Access to incubators tailored for women (Nigeria & North America)
  • Mentorship from global investors, sector experts, and diaspora leaders
  • Funding pipelines: angel networks, micro-VC, impact funds
  • Office space and hybrid working support
  • Ongoing peer accountability & community
