HERA Sri Lanka Accelerator

International Program

HERA SRI LANKA is more than an accelerator — it’s a reclamation ritual. Designed for women and women-identifying founders in Sri Lanka, it creates a powerful space for healing, rebuilding, and rising into leadership. HERA nurtures entrepreneurial confidence, creative expression, and business clarity through a cycle-aware, trauma-informed approach that honors the lived experiences of Sri Lankan women. It blends mentorship, practical skills, storytelling, and community support to help founders reclaim their voice, redefine success, and build ventures with purpose. HERA is where personal transformation meets entrepreneurial growth—fueling a new generation of bold, resilient women leaders.

HERA Nigeria Accelerator

International Program

HERA Nigeria is a 3-week intensive, trauma-informed, cycle-aware accelerator designed specifically for Nigerian women and women-identifying founders aiming to scale their ventures to North America. This immersive experience blends emotional resilience, business strategy, and leadership development to support founders on both a personal and entrepreneurial level. Through expert mentorship, market-entry guidance, pitch development, and community circles, participants gain the clarity, confidence, and practical tools needed to navigate North American ecosystems. HERA Nigeria creates a safe, empowering environment where women can heal, rebuild, and rise—turning their ideas into globally competitive ventures and stepping boldly into their leadership potential.

Accelerator for Students


The Accelerator for Students provides early-stage founders with the guidance, resources, and real-world experience needed to turn ideas into impactful ventures. Through expert mentorship, structured learning modules, and hands-on project development, students gain a deep understanding of how to validate concepts, build prototypes, and test solutions in real environments. The program fosters collaboration, creativity, and problem-solving, allowing young innovators to learn from peers and industry coaches. By the end of the accelerator, students not only strengthen their entrepreneurial mindset but also develop the confidence and skills required to lead meaningful projects, pitch their ideas, and pursue future opportunities with clarity and purpose.
